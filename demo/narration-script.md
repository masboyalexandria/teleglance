# TeleGlance — demo video narration script

Video: `teleglance-demo.mp4` — 65.7 s, 1280×720, 15 fps, silent (narration is read over it).
Recorded 25 Nov 2026 in the Even Hub simulator (Xvfb). Mock-backend shots use
clearly-labelled [DEMO] chats; the live section uses one real Telegram chat.
Total runtime is well under the 3-minute DoraHacks limit.

## Shot list (one idea per shot)

| Time | Shot | Narration |
|------|------|-----------|
| 0:00–0:04 | Title card | "TeleGlance — your Telegram, at a glance, on Even Realities G2 glasses." |
| 0:04–0:10 | Digest, 5 chats (mock) | "Five unread chats, one line each. The draft reply for the top chat is already written — you just approve it." |
| 0:10–0:22 | Three swipes (mock) | "Swipe to move between chats. No menus, no typing — glance, swipe, done." |
| 0:22–0:26 | Swipe to the last chat (mock) | "Swiping past the last chat opens it." |
| 0:26–0:31 | Message screen (mock) | "The full message, with the draft reply underneath, ready to send." |
| 0:31–0:37 | Tap to send (mock) | "One tap sends the draft. 'Reply sent.' — and the chat leaves the digest." |
| 0:37–0:41 | Double-tap dismiss (mock) | "Double-tap dismisses a chat you don't need. Nothing sent, nothing lost." |
| 0:41–0:45 | "Live proof" card | "That was demo data. Now the same app, on real Telegram data." |
| 0:45–0:51 | Live digest, 1 real chat | "One real unread chat — no demo tag. This is a live message, read straight from Telegram." |
| 0:51–0:58 | Live message screen | "Open it, and the real message is there, with its draft reply." |
| 0:58–1:02 | Back to live digest | "Back to the digest. Read and write, in seconds, without taking your phone out." |
| 1:02–1:06 | Outro card | "TeleGlance — open source at github.com/masboyalexandria/teleglance." |

## Notes for the voice-over

- Pace: calm, one sentence per shot; leave a half-second of silence at each cut.
- Pronunciation: "TeleGlance" = TEL-uh-glance. "G2" = "G-two".
- The video has no audio track; record the narration separately and mix it in,
  or submit the video silent with this script as the spoken guide.
- Do NOT re-record the send shot against the live backend: the tap-to-send
  footage is mock-only by design, so no real Telegram message is ever sent
  during filming.
